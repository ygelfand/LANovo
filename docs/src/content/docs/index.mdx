LANovo uses the display's existing microphones, speakers, camera and sensors. It connects to
Home Assistant through ESPHome and shares its software with
[EchoLocal](https://www.echolocal.link).

**Hardware:** Smart Display 10 (**blueberry**), Smart Display 8 (**amber**), Smart Display 7 (**ivy**),
JBL Link View (**opal**) and ThinkSmart View (**penghu**).
Unlocking is required. [Check your model and installation requirements](/start/devices/).

## Native dashboard and Tessera

<div class="showcase">
<div>

The [native dashboard](/use/dashboards/) combines your choice of clock face with weather and tabs
for lights, switches, cameras, doorbells and your home alarm. Add photo wallpaper, choose which
entities appear, and group controls by room. Sensor-equipped displays can rotate automatically.

[Tessera](/use/dashboards/#tessera) gives you custom pages designed in Home Assistant: climate,
energy, media, sensors, locks, covers, buttons and other tiles. Choose the native renderer or run
embedded Tessera firmware.

## Apps

<Image src={apps} alt="The app launcher: Media, Alarms, Calendar, Intercom, Cameras, Doorbell, Photos, Home alarm, Lights, Switches, Mirror, Radar, Radio and Shopping list" />

Open a [calendar or shopping list](/features/apps/), browse internet radio, watch animated weather
radar, control lights and switches, or open your Home Assistant cameras. Keep favourite apps in the
dock and drag to reorder them.

The [Home alarm](/features/home-alarm/) app includes a keypad, zones, arm/disarm controls and a
choice of designs. [Doorbells](/features/doorbell/) have event history and on-screen notifications.
[Photos](/features/photos/) can fill the screen or appear behind the clock, from Immich or Google
Photos.

[See the apps and their setup →](/features/apps/)

## Voice, music and casting

- **[Assist](/features/voice/):** on-device wake-word detection, independent Home Assistant
  pipelines, selectable feedback and a stop word without a wake word. Choose local or cloud
  pipelines; manage custom wake words and review requests with the optional integration.
- **[Music](/features/music/):** Home Assistant media playback and Music Assistant through
  Sendspin, with a mini-player and full-screen controls. Use the display as a Bluetooth speaker or
  mirror audio and video over AirPlay. Chromecast support is experimental.
- **[Visual feedback](/use/visuals/):** choose visuals for microphone input, speaker output and
  assistant states, with clocks, photos and visuals available as the screen saver.
- **[Timers and saved alarms](/features/messages/):** voice controls, repeating schedules, snooze,
  custom sounds, gentle volume and wake light. Automations can put messages on the screen.

## Calls, cameras and notifications

<div class="showcase flip">
<div>

[Intercom](/features/intercom/) makes audio and video calls between LANovo and EchoLocal devices.
Call from a browser, set auto-answer rules, or record a broadcast for the household.

The built-in [camera](/features/camera/) provides RTSP streams, ONVIF discovery and Home Assistant
snapshots. Physical microphone mute and the camera shutter remain in control.

[Doorbell events](/features/doorbell/), [messages](/features/messages/), timers and alarm-panel
alerts can appear over what is already on screen.

## Home Assistant and browser controls

<div class="showcase">
<div>

The [browser interface](/use/web/) manages settings, alarms, calls and broadcasts. Search settings,
download logs, export or import portable settings, or take live control of the screen. QR pairing
lets a phone control the screen or type into its keyboard.

ESPHome exposes settings, sensors and actions to Home Assistant. The optional
[EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/) adds device cards, activity and recordings, custom
wake words and tones, and Fleet comparison and settings sync across both projects.

The [Bluetooth LE proxy and iBeacon](/features/bluetooth/) support nearby BLE devices and room
presence. Sensor-equipped models also expose their proximity readings to Home Assistant.

## Hardware and installation

| Display | Codename | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Lenovo Smart Display 10 | **blueberry** | Supported |
| Lenovo Smart Display 8 | **amber** | Supported and tested |
| Lenovo Smart Display 7 | **ivy** | Supported |
| JBL Link View | **opal** | Supported |
| Lenovo ThinkSmart View | **penghu** | Supported; unlock guidance is on Discord |

You need Home Assistant, a supported display and USB access. Unlocking and firmware preparation
come before installing LANovo.

1. [Check the model and unlock requirements](/start/devices/).
2. [Prepare the display](/start/before/), then [install LANovo](/start/install/).
3. [Connect it to Home Assistant](/start/home-assistant/).

Updates and installation of older releases are available through Home Assistant, the display and
the [control harness](/features/updates/).
