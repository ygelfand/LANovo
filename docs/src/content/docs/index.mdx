## What LANovo is

Lenovo's Smart Displays have a good screen, stereo speakers, two far-field microphones, a camera
with a shutter, and a proximity sensor. Google stopped updating them.

LANovo replaces the whole Android stack with a single program that drives the screen, speakers,
microphones, camera and radios directly, and talks only to your Home Assistant.

## The EchoLocal integration

Optional, from HACS, and shared with LANovo's sister project [EchoLocal](https://www.echolocal.link): a card for
every display, a dashboard with all of them and your Echo devices, Fleet to compare and copy
settings between them, turn history with recordings, a wake word library, custom tones, groups and
health.

[The integration →](/start/integration/)
