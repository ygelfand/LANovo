## RTSP

Off by default. Turn it on with **Camera stream** in **Settings → Camera** or the **RTSP server**
switch (`rtsp`) in Home Assistant.

```text
rtsp://192.168.1.50:8554/main
rtsp://192.168.1.50:8554/sub
```

`main` is full resolution, `sub` is a smaller stream for previews and motion detection. Turn either
off, and set their resolutions, under **Settings → Camera → Streams**.

ONVIF discovery is supported.

## Snapshot

Home Assistant gets a camera entity with still pictures.

## Picture

**Settings → Camera** has a live preview and pages for **Picture**, **Exposure**, **White Balance**
and **Streams**, and **Reset to defaults**. Every setting is listed on
[Settings on the screen](/use/settings/#camera) and is also in Home Assistant. Camera settings
aren't copied by config sync.

## Shutter

With the shutter closed, the stream won't start, snapshots fail, and a privacy mark shows on screen.
Home Assistant's **Camera covered** sensor (`camera_covered`) follows it.


<Aside type="caution">
The RTSP stream has no password. Anyone on your network can watch it while it's on.
</Aside>
