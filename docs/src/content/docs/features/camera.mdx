## RTSP

Off by default. Turn it on with **Camera stream** in **Settings → Camera** or the **RTSP server**
switch in Home Assistant.

```text
rtsp://192.168.1.50:8554/main
rtsp://192.168.1.50:8554/sub
```

`main` is full resolution, `sub` is a smaller stream. ONVIF discovery is supported.

## Snapshot

Home Assistant gets a snapshot camera.

## Picture

**Settings → Camera** has picture, exposure and white balance settings with a live preview, and
**Reset to defaults**. These are also in Home Assistant.

## Shutter

With the shutter closed, the stream won't start, snapshots fail, and a privacy mark shows on screen.

<Aside type="caution">
The RTSP stream has no password.
</Aside>
