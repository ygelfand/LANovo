The grid in the dock opens the Apps screen. Tap the star on an app to keep it in the dock; drag to
reorder. **Media** and **Intercom** are in the dock to start with.

| App | |
|---|---|
| **Media** | The [player](/features/music/#player) |
| **Intercom** | [Calls](/features/intercom/) to other devices |
| **Alarms** | [Alarms](/features/messages/#alarms) with a wake light |
| **Radio** | Internet radio |
| **Radar** | Animated weather radar |
| **Calendar** | Upcoming events from Home Assistant calendars |
| **Shopping list** | A Home Assistant to-do list |
| **Mirror** | The camera, full screen |
| **Cameras** | Your Home Assistant cameras |
| **Lights**, **Switches** | Your [lights and switches](/use/dashboards/#lights-and-switches), full screen |
| **Photos** | Your [photos](/features/photos/#photos-app), full screen |
| **Doorbell** | Your [doorbells](/features/doorbell/) and their recent events |
| **Home alarm** | Your [alarm panel](/features/home-alarm/) from Home Assistant |

## Radio

Stations come from the **Radio Browser** integration, which you add in Home Assistant first. The
radio opens on **Local** and **Popular** stations; star a station to put it on the dial.

## Radar

An animated loop of rain and clouds around your home, with a short-range forecast. Under
**Settings → Dashboard → Weather**:

- **Radar source**: **Auto**, **NWS** (US only) or **RainViewer**
- **Radar starting range**: 50 to 800 miles, default 200

With the **Radar** [voice command](/features/voice/#commands) on, saying "radar" opens it.

## Calendar

Pick the calendars and **Days to show** (3, 7 or 14) under **Settings → Home control → Calendar**.

## Shopping list

Pick the to-do list under **Settings → Home control → Shopping list**. Add items with the on-screen
keyboard, tap to check them off, and turn on **Show completed items** to see the checked ones.

## Mirror

The camera, full screen. It's on displays with a camera. With the **Mirror**
[voice command](/features/voice/#commands) on, saying "mirror" opens it.

## Cameras

Pictures from your Home Assistant cameras, laid out to fill the screen. Swipe for the next page.
Tap a camera to see it full screen; tap again or the close button to go back.

Full screen, a camera that can stream plays live video, with a button for its sound (off to start
with). That needs the companion integration from [Voice triggers](/features/voice/#actions).
Otherwise the picture keeps updating as fast as Home Assistant sends it. An icon at the top shows
which you're getting.

The app needs Home Assistant actions allowed for the display. Its settings are under **Settings →
Home control → Cameras** and in Home Assistant:

| Setting | What it does | Choices | Default | Entity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dashboard tab | Adds a **Cameras** tab to the [native dashboard](/use/dashboards/#cameras) | On, Off | Off | `switch.<device>_dashboard_tab` |
| Select | Which cameras to show, by label, area or by hand | | All cameras | — |
| Thumbnail size | How big each picture is | 1–5 | 3 | `number.<device>_thumbnail_size` |
| Refresh | How often the pictures update | Off, 1–60 s | 5 s | `number.<device>_refresh` |
| Auto paginate | Turn to the next page every so often | Off, 5–120 s | Off | `number.<device>_auto_paginate` |
| Home Assistant URL | Where the display fetches the pictures. Found on your network on its own; set it if the app says Home Assistant's address isn't known | | | `text.<device>_home_assistant_url` |
