The grid in the dock opens the Apps screen. Tap the star on an app to keep it in the dock; drag to
reorder. **Media** and **Intercom** are in the dock to start with. Most apps close after three
minutes without a touch; **Photos** stays open.

## Media

Open **Media** for the full player: artwork, track information, playback controls and volume.
The mini-player keeps playback controls available while you use other screens. See
[Music and casting](/features/music/) for Home Assistant playback, Sendspin, Bluetooth and casting.

## Alarms

Create one-time or repeating alarms, choose a sound, set snooze and volume, or add a wake light.
The saved list is shared with the [browser alarm page](/use/web/#alarms) and the
[alarm voice commands](/features/voice/#alarm-and-call-controls).

<Image src={alarms} alt="Alarm editor with time, repeat, sound, snooze, volume and wake-light settings" />

## Intercom

The directory shows other LANovo and EchoLocal devices, with audio and video call buttons where
available. Open a device's information for more detail. See [Intercom](/features/intercom/) for
calls, broadcasts, auto-answer and browser calling.

## Radio

Stations come from the **Radio Browser** integration, which you add in Home Assistant first. The
radio opens on **Local** and **Popular** stations; star a station to put it on the dial.

The dial has playback and next/previous controls. **Local stations** and **Popular** open the
station browsers; choosing a station starts playback.

<Image src={radio} alt="The Radio app's Popular station browser" />

## Radar

An animated loop of rain and clouds around your home, with a short-range forecast. Under
**Settings → Dashboard → Weather**:

- **Radar source**: **Auto**, **NWS** (US only) or **RainViewer**
- **Radar starting range**: 50 to 800 miles, default 200

With the **Radar** [voice command](/features/voice/#commands) on, saying "radar" opens it.

## Calendar

Pick the calendars and **Days to show** (3, 7 or 14) under **Settings → Home control → Calendar**.

The app displays upcoming events from those Home Assistant calendars. Calendar selection and the
date range control what appears; event editing stays in your calendar service.

## Shopping list

Pick the to-do list under **Settings → Home control → Shopping list**. Add items with the on-screen
keyboard, tap to check them off, and turn on **Show completed items** to see the checked ones.

The list is shared with Home Assistant, not stored separately on the display. A phone can type
into **Add an item** through the [QR keyboard](/use/settings/#typing-with-a-phone).

<Image src={shopping} alt="Shopping list with an Add an item field and unchecked list items" />

## Lights and switches

These open the home-control lists full screen. Choose which entities appear under **Settings →
Home control**. The same selection feeds their [native dashboard tabs](/use/dashboards/#lights-and-switches).

## Photos

Browse the configured photo source full screen. The source can also supply wallpaper behind the
clock. See [Photos and wallpaper](/features/photos/) for Immich and Google Photos, fit modes and
transitions.

## Doorbell

Open the doorbell view and recent events. Notification behaviour and which doorbells appear are
configured under **Settings → Home control → Doorbell**. See [Doorbell](/features/doorbell/).

## Home alarm

Open the selected Home Assistant alarm panel, including its zones, keypad and supported actions.
The [Home alarm guide](/features/home-alarm/) shows the panel and every design in its picker.
Opening the app does not arm or disarm the panel.

## TV

The YouTube TV channel guide, and a remote for whatever YouTube TV is playing. It needs the
[YouTube TV integration](https://github.com/ygelfand/yttv-hacs) in Home Assistant, version 0.1.12 or
newer, and **Allow the device to perform Home Assistant actions** turned on for the display in the
ESPHome integration.

<div class="pair even">
<Image src={tv} alt="The TV app: the TVs along the top, then the channel guide with a red line at the time now" />
<Image src={tvCard} alt="A channel's card: the live picture, what's next, a favorite star and Watch here" />

- **The TVs** run along the top: every Chromecast the integration knows, with the display itself
  as **Here**. Tap one to pick it.
- **Below them** is what that TV is playing, with play/pause, stop and volume.
- **The guide** lists every channel, favorites first with a star, then A to Z. A line marks the
  time now. **Find a channel or show** narrows it.
- **Tap a show** that's on now to watch it on the chosen TV.
- **Tap a channel's name** for its card: what's on and what's next, a star to make it a favorite,
  and **Watch on** the chosen TV.

**Settings → Home control → YouTube TV**, or the settings button in the app, picks the
**Default TV** the app opens on and your **Favorites**.

## Mirror

The camera, full screen. It's on displays with a camera. With the **Mirror**
[voice command](/features/voice/#commands) on, saying "mirror" opens it.

## Cameras

Pictures from your Home Assistant cameras, laid out to fill the screen. Swipe for the next page.
Tap a camera to see it full screen; tap again or the close button to go back.

Full screen, a camera that can stream plays live video, with a button for its sound (off to start
with). That needs the companion integration from [Voice triggers](/features/voice/#actions).
Otherwise the picture keeps updating as fast as Home Assistant sends it. An icon at the top shows
which you're getting.

The app needs Home Assistant actions allowed for the display. Its settings are under **Settings →
Home control → Cameras** and in Home Assistant:

| Setting | What it does | Choices | Default | Entity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dashboard tab | Adds a **Cameras** tab to the [native dashboard](/use/dashboards/#cameras) | On, Off | Off | `switch.<device>_dashboard_tab` |
| Select | Which cameras to show, by label, area or by hand | | All cameras | — |
| Thumbnail size | How big each picture is | 1–5 | 3 | `number.<device>_thumbnail_size` |
| Refresh | How often the pictures update | Off, 1–60 s | 5 s | `number.<device>_refresh` |
| Auto paginate | Turn to the next page every so often | Off, 5–120 s | Off | `number.<device>_auto_paginate` |
| Home Assistant URL | Where the display fetches the pictures. Found on your network on its own; set it if the app says Home Assistant's address isn't known | | | `text.<device>_home_assistant_url` |
