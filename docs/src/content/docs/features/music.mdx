Turn each of these on under **Settings → Connected services**.

| Service | Switch | Displays |
|---|---|---|
| Media player | always on | all |
| Sendspin | `sendspin` | all |
| Chromecast | `cast_receiver` | all |
| AirPlay | `airplay` | all |
| Bluetooth speaker | `bluetooth_speaker` | all |

## Media player

The display is a Home Assistant **media player** called **Speaker**: radio, media sources, TTS
announcements and Music Assistant. Music ducks while the assistant listens and talks; **Music
ducking** (`media_duck_level`, −40 to −3 dB, default −15) in Home Assistant sets how far.


## Sendspin

A [Music Assistant](https://music-assistant.io) player for synchronized whole-home audio. Turn on
**Sendspin** and Music Assistant finds the display on its own; group it with other speakers there.

## AirPlay

Mirror a Mac, iPhone or iPad's screen and sound. Turn on **AirPlay**, then on the Mac or phone open
**Screen Mirroring** and pick the display by its name. The picture is sized to fit the screen and
turns with it. Stop mirroring from the Mac or phone, or turn AirPlay off.

AirPlay runs only while the switch is on.

## Chromecast

Turn on **Cast receiver** and the display shows up as a cast target in apps and in Google Home.
Each app runs its own receiver page, as it would on a Chromecast.

YouTube skips [SponsorBlock](https://sponsor.ajay.app) segments; pick which kinds under
**Cast receiver → Skip segments**, listed on [Settings on the screen](/use/settings/#cast-receiver).

<Aside type="caution" title="Experimental">
Apps that protect their video with DRM may not play.
</Aside>

## Bluetooth speaker

The display shows up under its own name. Pair a phone and play; the player shows the track and
cover art, and you can skip and pause from the screen.

## Sound machine

Two layers of steady sound, from Home Assistant: **Sound machine layer 1** and **layer 2**
(`noise_layer_1`, `noise_layer_2`). Choose Off, White, Pink, Brown, Rain, Ocean, Brook, Wind, Fire,
Crickets, Fan or Cabin for each; they mix together and duck for everything else. They don't survive
a restart.

## Player

A mini player shows on the dashboard while something plays. Tap it, or **Media** in the dock, for
the full player. Cast and AirPlay video play full screen; tap for controls.

**Settings → Display → Dashboard → Close paused player after** closes a paused player.
