The display is a Home Assistant **media player** called **Speaker**: radio, media sources, TTS
announcements and Music Assistant. Music ducks while the assistant listens and talks; **Music
ducking** (`media_duck_level`, −40 to −3 dB, default −15) in Home Assistant sets how far.


## Sendspin

Sendspin provides synchronized playback with
[Music Assistant](https://music-assistant.io).

1. Run Music Assistant on the same network and turn on **Settings → Connected services → Sendspin**
   on the display, or **Sendspin** (`sendspin`) in Home Assistant.
2. Select the display by its name in Music Assistant's players and play an album, playlist or
   radio station. Create the synchronized group in Music Assistant, not in the display's Settings.
3. On a screen, open **Media** for the track, artist, album and cover art. Play/pause, previous and
   next send commands back to the Sendspin server; seeking is not offered for Sendspin playback.

**Sendspin state** (`sendspin_state`) in Home Assistant helps check the connection:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `off` | Sendspin is disabled |
| `waiting` | Enabled and waiting for a server |
| `joined` | Connected to a server |
| `playing` | The server has started playback |

The state is a connection/playback diagnostic; a paused group can retain the `playing` state.
If it stays at `waiting`, check discovery between Music Assistant and the display. Turning
**Sendspin** off disconnects it and removes its network advertisement.

## AirPlay

Mirror a Mac, iPhone or iPad's screen and sound. Turn on **AirPlay**, then on the Mac or phone open
**Screen Mirroring** and pick the display by its name. The picture is sized to fit the screen and
turns with it. Stop mirroring from the Mac or phone, or turn AirPlay off.

## Chromecast

Turn on **Cast receiver**, open the Cast menu in a supported app, and select the display by name.


YouTube skips [SponsorBlock](https://sponsor.ajay.app) segments; pick which kinds under
**Cast receiver → Skip segments**, listed on [Settings on the screen](/use/settings/#cast-receiver).

## Bluetooth speaker

Pair the display in your phone's Bluetooth settings. Track information, cover art and playback
controls appear in **Media**.

## Sound machine

Play two background sounds together using **White noise layer 1** and **layer 2** in Home Assistant
(`noise_layer_1`, `noise_layer_2`). Choose None, White, Pink, Brown, Rain, Ocean, Brook, Wind, Fire,
Crickets, Fan or Cabin for each. Other audio reduces the background volume. Background sounds stop
when the display restarts.

## Player

A mini player shows on the dashboard while something plays. Tap it, or **Media** in the dock, for
the full player. Cast and AirPlay video play full screen; tap for controls.

**Settings → Dashboard → Close paused player after** closes a paused player.
