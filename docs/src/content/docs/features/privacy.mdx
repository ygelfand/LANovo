---
title: Privacy switches
description: The microphone switch and camera shutter.
---

Both are physical switches. Neither LANovo nor Home Assistant can change them.

## Microphone switch

Mutes the microphones. A tone plays when it changes.

## Camera shutter

Stops the camera: no stream, no snapshots.

## Indicators

While either is off, a red mark shows in a top corner: microphone on the left, camera on the right.
Turn them off with **Settings → Display → Privacy indicators**.

Home Assistant gets **Microphone muted** and **Camera covered** sensors.
