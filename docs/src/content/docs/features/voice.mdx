Wake words run on the display. Audio goes only where your Assist pipeline sends it.

## Two assistants

**Assistant 1** and **Assistant 2** each have their own wake word and Assist pipeline.

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. In Home Assistant, open the display's **Assistant 1** device.

2. Pick an **Assistant** (Assist pipeline) and a **Wake word**. Custom wake words added to Home
   Assistant are listed too.

3. The display downloads the wake word and starts listening.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
A slot with no wake word is off.
</Aside>

## On the display

**Settings → Voice assistants**, one page per assistant:

- **Talk now**: start without the wake word
- **Wake word sensitivity**: higher wakes more easily
- **Wake sound**: a chime, or none
- **Response playback**: **Stream** starts sooner and can stutter on a slow network; **Download
  first** waits for the whole answer
- **Visuals**: a visual for listening, waiting and responding, in the panel or full screen

## Stop

Tap the screen or say **"stop"**, no wake word needed. It stops, in order: a ringing timer, the
assistant, an announcement, music.

## Timers

"Set a timer for ten minutes" shows a countdown. See [Messages and timers](/features/messages/).
