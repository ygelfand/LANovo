When someone rings, the display wakes, rings and shows the door: live video with the doorbell's
sound, and a button to talk back. Up to four doorbells can be set up. Person, package, vehicle and
animal detections show as notifications.

## Add a doorbell

Open **Settings → Home control → Doorbell → Add doorbell**, or the gear on the **Doorbell** app.
The display looks in Home Assistant first, then offers three ways in:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Home Assistant (Reolink integration)** | Doorbells and cameras from Home Assistant's Reolink integration. Tap **Auto setup** on one; its camera and its ring, person, package, vehicle and animal sensors are filled in |
| **Reolink NVR** | Enter the NVR's address, user and password and tap **Connect**, then tap a channel |
| **Manual** | Enter the doorbell's name, UID or IP address, user and password |

A doorbell added with **Auto setup** has no password, so it uses Home Assistant for its video and
its rings, without talkback. Tap it on the Doorbell page and enter its password to connect to it
directly, with talkback.

**Save** checks the login first and says **Not saved** with the reason if it fails: a wrong
password, a different doorbell at that address, or a doorbell it can't reach.

Tap a doorbell on the Doorbell page to change it, see whether its events and video are working, or
**Remove** it.

## When someone rings

| Setting | Entity | What it does | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ring style | `select.<device>_camera_doorbell_ring_style` | What a ring does | Auto answer, Same as screen, None, Banner, Card, Full screen, Toast | Auto answer |
| Ring sound | `select.<device>_camera_doorbell_ring_sound` | | Chimes, Ring and custom tones | Ring |
| Ring for | `select.<device>_camera_doorbell_ring_for` | How long the ring sound repeats | Never, 5 s to 5 min | 30 s |
| Notification style | `select.<device>_camera_doorbell_notification_style` | How detections appear | Same as screen, None, Banner, Card, Full screen, Toast | Same as screen |
| Notification sound | `select.<device>_camera_doorbell_notification_sound` | | Chimes and custom tones | Ding |
| Dashboard tab | `switch.<device>_camera_doorbell_dashboard_tab` | A **Doorbell** tab on the [native dashboard](/use/dashboards/#doorbell-and-home-alarm) | | Off |

- **Auto answer** rings once and opens the live view with the doorbell's sound on.
- **Same as screen** uses the **Alert style** from [Alerts & Alarms](/use/settings/#alerts--alarms).
- **Banner**, **Card**, **Full screen** and **Toast** show "Someone's at the …" with a picture and a
  **Watch** button, while the ring sound repeats for **Ring for**.
- **None** shows nothing and plays nothing, but the screen still wakes.

Detections show as "Person at the …", "Package at the …", "Vehicle by the …" or "Animal at the …",
in the notification style, for 20 seconds.

## Watching and talking

The live view has a speaker button for the doorbell's sound and a microphone button: tap it to
talk, tap again to stop. The doorbell's sound is muted while you talk. An auto-answered view closes
after 2 minutes.

If the screen can't play the doorbell's main stream, it switches to the sub stream and says so.

The **Doorbell** app shows a picture of each doorbell and the recent events; tap one to watch.
Recent events are kept until LANovo restarts.

## Sources

Each doorbell picks where its video, events and talkback come from. **Automatic** suits most
doorbells; change them on the doorbell's page or in Home Assistant.

| Setting | Entity | Choices |
|---|---|---|
| Video | `select.<device>_camera_doorbell_1_video_source` | **Automatic**, Reolink, RTSP, Home Assistant |
| Events | `select.<device>_camera_doorbell_1_event_source` | **Automatic**, ONVIF, Reolink, Home Assistant |
| Talkback | `select.<device>_camera_doorbell_1_talkback_source` | **Automatic**, Reolink, ONVIF |
| Name | `text.<device>_camera_doorbell_1_name` | |

The same four entities exist for doorbells 2 to 4.

- **Automatic**, with a password, connects to a Reolink doorbell directly. If that fails, video
  tries RTSP and then Home Assistant, events use Home Assistant's sensors, and talkback tries ONVIF.
  Without a password it uses Home Assistant.
- **Home Assistant** video opens the doorbell's Home Assistant camera, with no talkback.
- **ONVIF** talkback works with doorbells that take G.711 audio.

**Doorbell status** (`doorbell_status`) in Home Assistant says how many doorbells are connected, or
what's wrong.

<Aside>
**Settings → Home control** shows the Doorbell page only when home control is on and **Allow the
device to perform Home Assistant actions** is turned on for the display in the ESPHome
integration. Without that, open the doorbell settings from the gear on the **Doorbell** app.
</Aside>
