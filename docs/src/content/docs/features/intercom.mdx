**Intercom** lists every LANovo display and every [EchoLocal](https://www.echolocal.link) device on
your network, found automatically. Open it from the dock or the Apps screen.

<Image src={intercom} alt="The Intercom directory, listing devices on the network" class="screen" />

## Calls

Tap the phone button for audio or the camera button for video. Video needs a camera on both ends.
During a call: mute, camera off, hang up.


## Answering

Tap to answer. Every option is under **Settings → Connected services → Intercom** and in Home
Assistant:

| Setting | | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Incoming calls** | Accept calls | On |
| **Auto-answer** | Answer automatically | Off |
| **Answer from** | Auto-answer **Everyone**, or **Selected**: the devices you pick under **Panels**, the Home Assistant users you pick under **Users**, and calls from the [web page](/use/web/#call) when **Auto-answer API calls** is on | Everyone |
| **Auto-answer with camera on** | Send video when auto-answering | Off |
| **Pause wake word during calls** | | On |
| **Video stream** | Main or sub stream | Main |
| **Ring**, **Video ring**, **Ringback** | Tones, including [custom ones](/features/tones/) | Ring, Ring, Ringback |

The **Call** and **Call with** sensors in Home Assistant show the call in progress.

You can also call by voice ("call the kitchen", see [Voice triggers](/features/voice/#actions)) or
from Home Assistant with the `call` action.

## Calling from Home Assistant

With the [EchoLocal integration](https://www.echolocal.link/start/integration/), the display's card
has a phone button: call the display from a dashboard in a browser or the Home Assistant app, with
audio or video. Sound and video go straight between the browser and the display, so the browser has
to be on the same network.

During a video call, the button beside hang-up, or a double-click on the picture, goes full screen.

The display sees who is calling by their Home Assistant user. To have it answer some of them on its
own, set **Answer from** to **Selected** and pick them under **Users**. The list shows the people in
Home Assistant that are linked to a user, under **Settings → People**.

## Device details

The info button on a device shows its model, whether it's in this home, its address and version,
and what it can do: calls, video calls, broadcasts, settings sync.

## Home key

Set the same **Home key** on every display under **Settings → System**, or with
`text.<device>_home_key` in Home Assistant. Devices with the same key are **In this home**; any
other device shows **Not in this home** and can't call this one, be called, or send it broadcasts.
Devices with no key at all count as one home. The key itself never crosses the network.
[Config sync](/use/configuration/#config-sync) copies it.

<Aside type="caution">
A device running an older version counts as not in this home once a key is set. Update every device
first.
</Aside>

## Broadcasts

A broadcast is a short voice message played on every other device in this home at once. Say
"broadcast" to your assistant and speak after the tone; it stops when you go quiet, after at most
30 seconds. Or say "broadcast dinner is ready" and the words are spoken for you. A ding says it was
sent, the failure sound that it wasn't.

The receiving devices chime, show who it's from, and play it. A device on a call or ringing an
alarm skips it. Turn **Receive broadcasts** (`switch.<device>_receive_broadcasts`, on by default)
off in Home Assistant to stop taking them.

From Home Assistant, the `broadcast` action takes a `message`: leave it empty to record from this
display's microphone, or give the address of an audio clip to send.
