The EchoLocal integration was made for LANovo's sister project, [EchoLocal](https://www.echolocal.link), and
works with LANovo displays too. One integration covers both: Lenovo Smart Displays and Echo devices
share its dashboard, cards and Fleet, side by side.

The display works in Home Assistant without this. The integration is an optional companion that
adds:

- **A card** for each display, with its settings and controls laid out by area
- **A dashboard** in the sidebar with every display, plus wake words, activity, groups, tones and
  health
- **Fleet**, which compares every device's settings with one of them and copies settings across
- What the ESPHome integration can't do on its own: a shared wake word library, custom tones and
  recordings of each turn
- [Voice actions](/features/voice/#voice-triggers) such as "call the kitchen" and "broadcast
  dinner is ready", and live video in the [Cameras app](/features/apps/#cameras)
- [Calls to a display](/features/intercom/#calling-from-home-assistant) from a dashboard

## Install

Needs Home Assistant **2026.8** or newer.

<Steps>

1. In HACS, open **⋮ → Custom repositories**, add `https://github.com/ygelfand/echolocal-hacs` with
   the category **Integration**.

   [![Open in HACS](https://my.home-assistant.io/badges/hacs_repository.svg)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/hacs_repository/?owner=ygelfand&repository=echolocal-hacs&category=Integration)

2. Install **EchoLocal**, then restart Home Assistant.

3. **Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → EchoLocal**.

</Steps>

Its only option is **Show the EchoLocal dashboard in the sidebar**. Change it later from the
integration's **Configure**.

## The card

Each card is drawn like its display, Smart Display 10, 8 or 7, JBL Link View or ThinkSmart View.
The buttons beside it open a panel of settings for each area: playback, screen, posters, camera and
each assistant. Every setting has a **?** beside it that says what it does. The screen shows the
display's clock in its theme and brightness, and the bar along its bottom edge lights while the
assistant listens and replies. The phone button
[calls the display](/features/intercom/#calling-from-home-assistant).

<Image src={cardPanel} alt="The card for a Smart Display 10: the display drawn with its clock and date, buttons for each area, and call, activity, settings and diagnostics" class="screen" />

Add one to any dashboard: **Edit dashboard → Add card → EchoLocal Satellite**, then pick the device.

```yaml
type: custom:echolocal-satellite-card
device_id: 0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef
help: true
```

| Option | Does |
|---|---|
| `device_id` | The display |
| `help` | Show the **?** beside each setting. On by default |

## The dashboard

**EchoLocal** in the sidebar.

### Home

Every display's card on one page, or one section per group. Each group's bar has **Mute all**, **Ring** and **Stop** where its devices have them, which act on every device in it. If only some of them take it, the bar says how
many did.

### Activity

Every turn a device reports: what woke it, what it heard, what it answered, and how long listening,
thinking and replying took. Where a recording was kept, play back what the microphones sent. How
many recordings each assistant keeps is in its panel on the card.

### Wake words

Which device listens for what, and the wake word library every device can use. Drop a microWakeWord
`.tflite` file onto the tab to add it; it's offered to every display straight away, with no
restart. The library lives in Home Assistant's `config/custom_wake_words/`.

### Groups

A table of devices against groups. Tick a box to put a device in a group, rename a group in its
heading, or add and delete groups. Groups are Home Assistant labels, so they also work in
automations and in Home Assistant's own filters.

### Fleet

Compare every device's settings with one of them, and copy settings from it to the others. Only
administrators see this tab. LANovo displays and [EchoLocal](https://www.echolocal.link) devices
appear in the same table; a setting one of them doesn't have shows as not applying.

<Image src={fleet} alt="The Fleet tab: a Primary picker, settings as angled columns grouped by section, and one row per device marking each setting as matching, differing or not applying" class="screen-wide" />

- **Primary** is the device the others are compared with.
- Each setting is a column, grouped by section, and each device a row. A cell says whether the
  device matches the primary, differs from it, or doesn't have that setting, such as a screen setting
  on an Echo Dot.
- With **Show matching** off, only the settings that differ on some device are shown.
- Collapse a section to a single column that counts how many settings differ. Its checkbox ticks or
  clears the whole section.
- The pills above the table limit it to the devices in one group.
- The eye beside a device leaves it out of the comparison until the page is reloaded.
- **Local** is a list of settings that are never synced, for ones you want to keep different on each
  device. Search it and tick a setting to add it.

To copy, tick the settings and the devices, then press **Sync**. Each device's row then says how many
settings changed, or what went wrong.

<Image src={fleetSync} alt="Fleet with two devices ticked, the Sync button ready, and the Local list open with a search for auto" class="screen-wide" />

Each assistant's pipeline and wake word are compared and set through Home Assistant's own selects.
A device on firmware from before Fleet shows **needs an update** and can't be synced; an offline one
is greyed out.

### Tones

Upload a sound, tidy it up, and send it to devices. It then shows up in their sound pickers for
chimes, timers, alarms, rings and the wake tone. See [Custom tones](/features/tones/).

### Health

One row per device: version, update status, Wi‑Fi signal, CPU temperature, load, memory, disk and
address.

## Actions

| Action | Does |
|---|---|
| `echolocal.assign_tone` | Send a tone from the library to devices (`tone`, `device_id`) |
| `echolocal.unassign_tone` | Remove a tone from devices; it stays in the library |

<Aside>
Each display's own actions, such as messages, alarms and timers, come from the ESPHome
integration. See [Messages, timers and alarms](/features/messages/).
</Aside>
