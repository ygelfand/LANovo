## Visuals

Choose from 33 visuals that react to the microphone, the speaker, or both. Show one full screen, or two
side by side or stacked.

[All 33 visuals →](/use/visuals/)

The [visual gallery](/use/gallery/) also shows every clock face, UI style, alert layout and timer
layout with its picker name.

## Settings

**Settings → Display → Screen saver**:

- **Start after**: 30 seconds, 1, 2, 5 or 15 minutes, or never
- **Visual 1** and **Visual 2**: a visual, [Photos](/features/photos/#screen-saver), or None
- **Visual 1 audio** and **Visual 2 audio**: microphone, speaker or both
- **Clock face**, **position**, **alignment** and **size**, or no clock
- **Weather**: the screen saver's own weather card and layout
- **Visual label**: text some visuals show
- **Night mode**: below

With a [Tessera dashboard](/use/dashboards/#tessera), **Settings → Dashboard → Screen saver** picks
this screen saver (**Native**) or Tessera's.

Every option, with its default and Home Assistant entity, is on
[Settings on the screen](/use/settings/#screen-saver).

## Night mode

**Screen saver → Night mode**:

- **Mode**: **Dim**, **Dim to a clock** (the default), **Turn off the screen**, or **Nothing**
- **Darker than**: room brightness, in lux (default 5)
- **Starts after**: how long it has to be that dark (default 5 minutes)

## Waking

Tap the screen. With **Wake on motion** on, walking up to the display wakes it; **Detection range**
sets the detection sensitivity.

**Sleep** in the dock starts the screen saver.
