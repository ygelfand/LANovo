## Visuals

33 visuals, each reacting to the microphone, the speaker, or both. Show one full screen, or two
side by side or stacked.

[All visuals →](/use/visuals/)

## Settings

**Settings → Display → Screen saver**:

- **Mode**: **Native** (LANovo's clock and visuals) or **Tessera** (the screen saver set up in
  Tessera)
- **Start after**: 30 seconds, 1, 2, 5 or 15 minutes, or never
- **Visual 1** and **Visual 2**: a visual, or None
- **Visual 1 audio** and **Visual 2 audio**: microphone, speaker or both
- **Clock face**, **position**, **alignment** and **size**, or no clock
- **Weather**: show the weather card

These are also in Home Assistant.

## Night mode

**Screen saver → Night mode**:

- **Mode**: **Dim**, **Dim to a clock**, **Turn off the screen**, or **Nothing**
- **Darker than**: room brightness, in lux
- **Starts after**: how long it has to be that dark

## Waking

Tap the screen. With **Wake on motion** on, walking up to the display wakes it.

**Sleep** in the dock starts the screen saver.
