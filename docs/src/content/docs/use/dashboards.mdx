The dashboard is what the display shows when you're using it. There are two kinds, and you pick one
in **Settings → Display → Dashboard**:

- **Native**: a big clock, the weather, and tabs for your lights, switches and cameras
- **Tessera**: pages of tiles you lay out in Home Assistant

## Native

The native dashboard is a clock with the date, an optional weather card, and optional
[wallpaper from Immich](/features/photos/) behind it. Pick the clock face, position, size and color
under **Settings → Display → Dashboard → Clock**.

### Lights and switches

Open **Settings → Home control → Native → Lights** (or **Switches**) and turn on **Enable**; the
dashboard gains a **Lights** (or **Switches**) tab. **Select devices** picks what goes on it by
label, by area or one by one, and **Group by area** sets how it's grouped:

- Every device on its own card
- Areas with a few devices on one card
- Every area on one card

<Image src={lights} alt="The Lights tab, with a card per room" class="screen" />

The same cards open full screen from the **Lights** and **Switches** apps on the Apps screen.

### Cameras

Turn on **Settings → Home control → Cameras → Dashboard tab** and the dashboard gains a **Cameras**
tab with the pictures from the [Cameras app](/features/apps/#cameras). Tap a camera to watch it full
screen.

### Weather

Pick a Home Assistant weather entity under **Settings → Home control → Weather**, then choose how it
looks on the dashboard and on the screen saver: Compact, Stack, Card, Detail, Icon or Words, with
or without animation, in the weather's colors or the theme's, and where it sits. The dashboard and
the screen saver each have their own layout.

**Weather alerts** replaces the card with severe and extreme US warnings from the National Weather
Service until you dismiss them. Every weather setting is on
[Settings on the screen](/use/settings/#weather).

## Tessera

[Tessera](https://github.com/MaxGramser/homeassistant_espscreen) is a Home Assistant integration
with a drag-and-drop editor for touch screens. LANovo speaks its screen protocol, so the
display shows up in Tessera like any other screen. Lay out the pages there; the display draws
them in your style and theme.

### Set it up

<Steps>

1. Install Tessera in Home Assistant from its
   [GitHub page](https://github.com/MaxGramser/homeassistant_espscreen).

2. On the display, set **Settings → Display → Dashboard** to **Tessera**.

3. The display appears in Tessera as a screen. Without tiles it shows:

   <Image src={setup} alt="A screen asking to be set up in Tessera" class="screen-wide" />

4. Add pages and tiles in Tessera's editor. They show up on the display.

</Steps>

The Smart Display 10, Smart Display 8 and ThinkSmart View appear as two screens, one for landscape
and one for portrait, so you can lay each out for the way the display is standing. The others
appear as one.

**Settings → Home control → Tessera** sets the tile grid, which orientations to offer, and whether
the display goes back to the first page after the screen saver. The screen saver can be a Tessera
page too: **Settings → Display → Screen saver → Mode**.

<Aside type="tip">
Turn on **Allow the device to perform Home Assistant actions** on the display's ESPHome device in
Home Assistant, or taps on tiles won't do anything. The Home control page reminds you if it's off.
</Aside>

## Rotation

The Smart Display 10, Smart Display 8 and ThinkSmart View rotate automatically. The
dashboards, settings, camera and everything else turn with them.
