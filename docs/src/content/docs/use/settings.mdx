The gear in the dock opens Settings. Most settings are also in Home Assistant; see
[Configuration](/use/configuration/).

<Image src={settings} alt="The Settings page" class="screen" />

## Network & internet

Wi‑Fi network, IP and MAC address, router, lease. Change networks with
[`./lanovo wifi`](/start/install/#changing-wifi-later).

**Verify certificates** checks certificates on downloads (updates, wake words, photos).

## Connected services

- **Sendspin**: Music Assistant player
- **Cast receiver**: Chromecast, with per-app settings ([Music and casting](/features/music/))
- **Intercom**: incoming calls and auto-answer ([Intercom](/features/intercom/))
- **Bluetooth proxy**: BLE proxy for Home Assistant
- **Bluetooth speaker**

## Display

- **Brightness**, **Adaptive brightness**
- **Theme**, **Style**, **Size** ([Styles and themes](/use/styles/))

  <Image src={theme} alt="The theme picker" class="screen" />

- **Dashboard**: Native or Tessera, clock, weather, wallpaper, **Close paused player after**
  ([Dashboards](/use/dashboards/))
- **Screen saver**: visuals, clock, night mode, wake on motion ([The screen saver](/use/screen-saver/))
- **Dock position**, **Hide dock after**
- **Volume panel position**
- **Privacy indicators** ([Privacy switches](/features/privacy/))

## Home control

Home control on/off, dashboard and screen saver mode, native lights and switches, Tessera grid,
weather entity, shopping list, calendars.

## Sound

Volumes for media, assistant, alarms and system sounds; system sounds; notification sound.

## Alerts & Alarms

Alert style, chime per tone, timer style, timer sound, timer ring time
([Messages and timers](/features/messages/)).

## Voice assistants

A page per assistant ([Voice assistants](/features/voice/)).

## Camera

Live preview, streams, picture, exposure, white balance, **Camera stream**, **Reset to defaults**
([Camera](/features/camera/)).

## System

- **Language**
- **Date & time**: time zone and 24-hour format
- **Keyboard size**
- **Config sync** ([Configuration](/use/configuration/#config-sync))
- **Software update** ([Updates](/features/updates/))
- **Restart**: the app or the device

## Developer options

**ADB over network** (an unauthenticated root shell on your network), a visuals gallery, and visual
settings.

## About

Name, version, build date.
